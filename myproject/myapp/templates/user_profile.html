Cancellation and refund policy

You can cancel an order within 3 calendar days of placing it, only while delivery status is pending. Cancellation is not available after packing, shipping, out-for-delivery or delivery starts. Online payment refunds are processed within 5–7 business days after cancellation; cash-on-delivery orders have no payment refund.
